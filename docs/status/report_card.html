How the scores work

  • Loading: current bundles and collections that are not yet in the registry, of all current ones.
  • File paths: products, collections and bundles with an invalid file_ref, of the node's visible products.
  • Fidelity: visible products without ancestry (missing from their collection's and bundle's /members) that the node must fix, or that need a closer look, of the node's visible products. Products still within the grace period are not counted.
  • Registry Health (EN and the sweepers team, not the node): products without ancestry that EN or the sweepers team fix, collections stamped without a link to their bundle, and index mapping errors. A mapping error on a core or ops: field is red; a type conflict on another field is yellow.
  • Green is no issues. Yellow is some issues, but below 5% of the node's items. Red is 5% or more of the items, which is enough to harm users of the registry. A node's status is the worst of Loading, File paths and Fidelity.
