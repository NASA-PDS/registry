Every Index Health issue is EN's or the sweepers team's to fix: collections the sweepers stamped before their products were loaded, products a node overwrote that the sweepers never revisited, collections without a link to their bundle, loaded collections the sweepers never processed, and index mappings. A node status marked * also has open Index Health issues; they are not counted in the node status.

How the scores work

  • Loading: current bundles and collections that are not yet in the registry, of all current ones.
  • File paths: products, collections and bundles with an invalid file_ref, of the node's visible products.
  • Fidelity (the node's): visible products without ancestry (missing from their collection's and bundle's /members) that the node must fix, or that need a closer look, of the node's visible products. These are a collection that is not loaded, an inventory that lists another version of the product or omits it, and a collection whose inventory lists the product but whose refs do not. Products still within the grace period are not counted.
  • Index Health (EN's problems and the sweepers team's, not the node's): collections the sweepers stamped before their products were loaded and never revisited, and products a node overwrote that the sweepers never revisited (EN runs the ancestry orphan repair for both); collections without a link to their bundle (EN runs the bundle-link pass); loaded collections the sweepers never processed; products the sweepers team owns; and index mapping problems. Only the fields the API relies on count (lid, lidvid, vid, the archive status, superseded_by and ancestor_refs): one that is missing from the node's mapping, or mapped with a different type from the other nodes (text and keyword count as the same), makes the score red. Other mapping differences are ignored.
  • Green is no issues. Yellow is some issues, but below 5% of the node's items. Red is 5% or more of the items, which is enough to harm users of the registry. A node's status is the worst of Loading, File paths and Fidelity, which are the node's. Index Health is EN's and is shown beside the node status, not in it.
